Why do C readings reverse R readings?

With a nominal 24 VAC control transformer, R is the 24-volt supply leg and C is the return. If a thermostat closes the R-to-Y circuit, the Y terminal rises to approximately R potential. Thus R–Y ≈ 0 VAC but C–Y ≈ 24 VAC. If Y is off, the connected equipment load normally pulls Y toward C: R–Y ≈ 24 VAC and C–Y ≈ 0 VAC.

Actual readings vary with transformer voltage, control-board electronics, floating terminals and connected loads. Never jumper arbitrary terminals or infer a real wiring fault from this simplified chart alone.
