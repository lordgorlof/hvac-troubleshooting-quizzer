Key: ↑ High   ↓ Low   Normal = expected reading   SH = Superheat   SC = Subcooling   RH control = Humidity control / evaporator icing. These are relative diagnostic patterns, not numerical target readings.

Study notes

Guide markers 1–5 and entries such as “Normal4” or “↓/” are preserved as printed in the master table. Consult the source guide for qualifications. RH observations such as Ice, Poor and Normal describe humidity/icing performance rather than a measured relative-humidity percentage.

Different faults can produce similar readings. Confirm diagnosis using operating conditions, airflow, manufacturer targets, line temperatures, and temperature drops across suspected restrictions.
